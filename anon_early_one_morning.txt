Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The lyrics were first published in 1787, and the melody was first published
by William Chappell in his National English Airs, around 1855-1859.

The tempo of the Norbert Neunzling recording and the MIDI are ~76 bpm.

Lyrics
~~~~~~

There are many versions of the lyrics of this popular folk song. A broadside
ballad sheet from 1828/9 titled it "The Lamenting Maid". This version of the
lyrics comes from the News Chronicle Songbook, 1956.

Early one morning,
Just as the sun was rising,
I heard a young maid sing,
In the valley below.

CHORUS:

Oh, don't deceive me,
Oh, never leave me,
How could you use
A poor maiden so?

Remember the vows,
That you made to your Mary,
Remember the bow'r,
Where you vowed to be true,

Chorus

Oh Gay is the garland,
And fresh are the roses,
I've culled from the garden,
To place upon thy brow.

Chorus

Thus sang the poor maiden,
Her sorrows bewailing,
Thus sang the poor maid,
In the valley below.

Chorus
