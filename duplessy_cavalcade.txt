RASGUEADO :

In this piece, the rasgueados are played in 6/8.
Let's take the part from bar 192 to 196 :

The strumming base is made with p & i :
8th notes, alternating the 2 fingers,
strum down with i, up with p
Then you add the rasgueado in, which is
a m i down, p up.

The rythm goes like this :
 i   p   i   a    m    i   p

PERFORMANCE NOTES :

Overall, the 3 versions I've linked on top have vastly different nuances,
dynamics and fingerings. I didn't write much of them, so I encourage you
to listen to different recordings to make up your mind.

For my part, I based it more off Vera's version and tried to go for the easiest
fingerings. I'll mention where it might be worth looking at other ones.

Also, the Intro is rarely played.

BAR 3 - You can slide 4 before arpeggiating the chord. In the score,
        the arpeggio is written as 32nd notes.

BAR 4 - A good separation of bass and melody is in order.

BAR 5 - Keep in mind that the grace note doesn't count as a quarter note.

BARS 9,10 -Be sure to play legato.

BAR 11 - Don't accentuate the bass; play with the flesh of your thumb.

BAR 22 - On the score, the picking pattern is p i m i m p i m i m i m ...
         which is fine, but using the a finger makes more sense to me.

BAR 37 - There are a lot of ways to strum the last chord. Mathias arpeggiates
         with p, then strums up with p. Vera does a rasgueado.

BAR 43 - You may be tempted to barre on III - don't :)

BAR 49 - Anchoring the i finger makes it easier.
