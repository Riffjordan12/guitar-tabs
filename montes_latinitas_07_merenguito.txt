Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The sheet tempo is 145 beats per minute (eighth notes), but the Irina Kircher
reference recording and others play it at twice the speed, so im assuming the
sheet is wrong and it should be 145 bpm (quarter notes)? Has anyone heard the
Alfonso Montes CD which accompanied the sheet music?

In the sheet there is an opening repeat sign at the start of measure 11, but
no closing repeat sign. Most recordings I've listened to don't repeat any of
the sections (though one repeats measures 1-10).

The sheet indicates that the notes in measure 51 are to be held (allowed to
ring, not cut off), and are then repeated (along with the top E) as a chord
in measure 52, but I haven't heard any recordings play the chord.

left hand fingering (LHF) below the tab -
1 - index finger (forefinger)
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger (annular)
4 - little finger (pinky)

===      - note held, length of note, duration
bpm      - beats per minute
cresc.   - crescendo - growing louder
decresc. - decrescendo - growing softer
f        - forte - loud, strong
mf       - mezzo-forte - moderately loud
mp       - mezzo-piano - moderately quiet
p        - piano - quiet, softly
