Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1) This tab transcription is based exclusively on Karl Marino's performance.
2) The chords that demand acrobatic strechings are easier to play if the left
   hand fingers are positioned first on the bass notes (this will be the anchor)
   and only then on the trebles. The chords that involve using the left hand's
   thumb for example are slowly arpeggiated so the player will have enough time
   to place his left hand fingers on every note beginning with those that are
   first played (the bass notes).

left hand fingering   
t - thumb    
1 - index finger        
2 - middle finger       
3 - ring finger        
4 - little finger         

C(n)   barre on the nth fret                                               
c(n)   part barre on the nth fret
[      part barre with the index finger

arp  - arpeggiate
r.h. - play the harmonics with the right hand 

h    - hammer-on
p    - pull-off
/    - slide up
\    - slide down
g    - grace note (ornament) - extra notes played immediately 
       before the main notes

<x>   - natural (open) harmonic on the x fret
x[y]  - artificial harmonic, play x fret, point to the y fret
