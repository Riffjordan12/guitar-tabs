Notes & Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Andrea Morricone is the son of Ennio Morricone.

measure 27 - in the sheet the B on the 2nd beat is on the 2nd string (not 3rd)

measures 28,40 - if the stretch on the 1st beat is too much, maybe play the
                 bottom F# an octave higher (on the 4th string 4th fret), or
                 you could try playing the F# on the 2nd fret with your thumb
                 from above the fret board - needs must!

measure 44 - in the sheet the A & D in the 1st chord are half notes

===    - notes held, length of note, duration
C2     - barre on the 2nd fret
F.     - Fermata - notes held for longer than their value
