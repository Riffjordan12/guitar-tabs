left hand fingering (LHF)
1 - index finger
2 - middle finger
3 - ring finger
4 - little finger

C(n)   barre on the nth fret

h  - hammer-on
p  - pull-off
/  - slide up
\  - slide down
g  - grace note (ornament) - an extra note played before the main note -
     the next note is usually played as a hammer-on, pull-off or slide

Tab and left hand fingering by Jim Kirby.
Last updated 30 May 2021 - see Notes below.

Fingerings are the ones I use and am comfortable with.
Numbers in brackets are suggested alternate fingerings, like so... [3]
Feel free to use your own fingering.

Time Signature -  2/2 Capital C with a line thru it.
Count -  1 e & a 2 e & a

====== means let the note ring, or sustain.

The first few measures have the count to get you going.
The first measure is a pickup which is played at the end of the count.
Count -  ... 1 e & a 2 e da da. The "da da" are the first 2 notes, 1-3.
Continue right into the 2nd measure and keep on going.
