Notes and Legend
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
"Cordao de Ouro (Gold Chain) is a Lundu de Capoeira dedicated to the great
guitarist / composer Baden Powell. The Lundu and the Modhina were the first
identifiable styes of brazilian popular music. Whereas the Modhina descended
from the upper to lower classes, the Lundu rose from the popular classes to
the bourgeoisie and was the first form of 'black' music to be accepted by the
aristocracy. Through the Lundu, the black man gave Brazilian music its
essential elements."
